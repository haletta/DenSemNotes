Catch-up

Press Record when class begins. Every 10 minutes the part you just heard is summarized here with its details, and when you pause or finish, everything becomes one summary of the whole lecture.

Note helper

    While the lecturer talks, key points, quotes, ideas from books and authors, key terms, examples, questions and due dates appear here, newest at the bottom. Press + to put one in your notes.

    Ask anything about your material. Answers come from your own notes first, then the lecture summaries, questions asked in class and the transcript, with numbered sources you can click.
